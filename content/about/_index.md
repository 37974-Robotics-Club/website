We're a group of Edmonton students who build, code and drive a robot for *FIRST* Tech Challenge. In September 2026 our families incorporated 37974 Robotics Club as a not-for-profit so the team could raise money, hold insurance and keep its own equipment.

FTC teams design, build and program a robot to play a new game each season, competing in alliances against other teams from across the province. Judges also award teams for their engineering process, outreach and teamwork, so our journal and community work matter as much as the robot.

Students make the decisions. Adults teach, supervise and keep us safe.
