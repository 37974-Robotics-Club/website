  roles:
    - name: Mechanical
      text: Upon analysis of the game manual, mechanical develops a plan for the design of the robot, and throughout the season, builds the robot according to game guidelines. A great majority of construction is done by mechanical, as well as location of materials, so that the CADed robot may come into place.
    - name: Programming
      text: The programming team brings the robot to life; they develop the code that allows the robot to perform during matches. This includes motors, servos, sensors, cameras, and more. They test and improve code throughout the season to improve robot performance.
    - name: CAD
      text: The CAD team deals with the digital model of the robot created using computer-aided design software. Our team uses Onshape to verify designs and ideas digitally before handing it off to the mechanical team.
    - name: Outreach

Founded in 2026, "Subject to Change" is a parent-led team consisting of students from Edmonton, Alberta who build, code and drive a robot for _FIRST_ Tech Challenge. Through our participation in the First Tech Challenge, we work towards inspiring youth to a future in STEM and providing a multitude of opportunities to explore their passions. Each of our members have a specified role given that suits their strengths and interests, with chances to learn and grow in other areas. 

FTC teams design, build and program a robot to play a new game each season, competing in alliances against other teams from all around. Judges also award teams for their engineering process, outreach and teamwork, so our outreach and community work is just as valued.
