We are FTC Team 37974 "Subject to Change", a community team based in Edmonton, Alberta. Team 37974 is a brand new team for the BIOBUZZ season, but many of our members had previously competed together on the Telus World of Science robotics teams 12094 and 24791. Upon their temporary closure due to flooding, many of us decided to regroup and try to build this team from the ground up. Previously, our members had competed at the international level with teams from all around the world, including at the FIRST Tech Challenge Canadian Rockies Premier Event @ KDays in both 2025 and 2026. 

Being a part of FIRST means Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition are a big part of our team values, the key principles that emphasise competition built on respect and kindness. No matter the situation, it is always important to treat everyone with the respect that they deserve. Even while competing, it can often look like lending parts to another team, or sharing expertise in a certain area. We should all strive for a community based around a shared passion for robotics, connecting with others and sharing that knowledge. 

There are currently 12 students on our team, ranging from Grades 8 to 12. Given our wide range of strengths and interests, each member has a specified role in a primary and secondary team, allowing them the chance to learn and grow in different areas. Our members have a range of experience in FTC, with this being the first season for some, while for others their fourth.  

During our first season together as a team, it is our hope to grow and inspire through our creativity, innovation and passion, encouraging curiosity within the greater STEM community.  We wish to empower students through meaningful STEM education that will allow them the opportunity to explore the many possibilities that science has to provide. Throughout the journey of discovery through experimentation, students will be given the chance to acquire new perspectives, growing this shared passion in ways they didn't think possible. 

This blog will provide updates regarding competitions we compete in, build updates, outreach events, etc. Anyone looking to connect further with the team or potentially support us should head to the "Contact Us" section, where further details are provided.
