Our Robot has been in the works for over a month now and we have had a few Technical difficulties but we worked through them.  We started Our Robot by Cading it out.   After we finished the cad we moved on to assembly. This was a area were we had some set backs.  One of these set backs was our Shooter.  The problem with the shooter was the transfer system.  The shooter could only fire when we had more then one ball in the shooter.  This was caused by because the transfer system could not push the ball far Enough into the shooter.  This problem was solved by repositioning the transfer system and making the intake we were using on it longer.  Even though we ran into a few problems along the way, we were able to work together and figure them out.
