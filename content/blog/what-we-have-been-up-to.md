With our first meet coming up soon, the team has been working harder than ever on our robot. We set out with the goal of having a working pollen-only bot ready for the November 21st Bison league Meet one, and are currently on track to meet that goal. We have made some significant progress as of late with the completion of our drivetrain, game element intake and sorting mechanism, as well as a turreted pollen shooter.
