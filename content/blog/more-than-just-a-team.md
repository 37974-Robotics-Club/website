Throughout the last two months of my life, I have learned more lessons than any other point in my education. Whether it be broad or niche I know for certain that joining Subject to Change has had a profound, positive impact on my life. As the only rookie member of the team I had countless worries racing through my head, _would I fit in, would I be a drag on the team, how can I help if I know nothing about robotics?_  Despite my apprehension; I did join the team, and stuck with my decision. Now -- two months later, because of my teammates, coaches and everybody involved in the team that helped me along my journey of learning how to effectively participate in FTC. I can now say with confidence that I am a member of Subject to Change and that I belong.
