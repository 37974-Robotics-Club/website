## Goal

This meeting, as well as the next few, were the final stretch before our first meet on the 21st, and so there was huge priority on intake assembly this time. 

## What we tried

Intake assembly was continued and the chassis was completed. The drive team took the robot for a test drive, looking to find any issues that could possibly come up in competition. Besides building, cable management on the robot also needed to be dealt with, ensuring that everything was properly organised. 

## Results

While testing the intake, it was found that the surgical tubing initially used was not effective at pulling the pollen, due to its lack of grip and resistance on the object. There were also found to be several tolerance issues in the drivetrain. 

## Decision

Alternate solutions and materials needed to be found for the intake, so several alterations needed to be made to ensure sufficient grip. 

## Next steps

The next meeting would see further testing of the effectiveness of the intake.
