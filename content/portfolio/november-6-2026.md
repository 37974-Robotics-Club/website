## Goal

The main goal this meeting was to just get as far as we could in the assembly process with the limited time we had, with other members working on things such as coding. 

## What we tried

The coders worked on the limelight calibration, using it to find the distance from the hive, while also making a test autonomous for 4 different starting positions. Vector wheels were built, and as chassis assembly continued on, pressure fit bearings for the side panels were worked on. While CAD for the nectar shooter was progressing, the outreach team made some banners for socials.
