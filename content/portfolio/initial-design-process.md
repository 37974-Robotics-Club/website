## Goal

For the first week, the initial goal was to plan out a master sketch and design for the robot, considering all aspects discussed in the season kickoff a week prior.  

## What we tried

On Onshape, members brainstormed the design of the robot and started the process of making it come to life online. Other members created a rough drivetrain assembly. 

## Decision

Team branding was one thing that was mainly finalised, settling on the team colours of navy blue and gold. From there, members started brainstorming the logo design, contemplating on how to incorporate the main values and ideas into it, all while ensuring it still looked aesthetically pleasing. 

## Next steps

Thinking ahead, it was decided that the next meeting should be focused on getting basic necessities for the new season set up, ensuring each member had the materials and equipment needed to get started with each of their roles for the upcoming season.
