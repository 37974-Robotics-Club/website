## Problem

The v1 claw took about 3 seconds per pickup and dropped one piece in five.

## What we tried

1. Rigid rollers with foam: grabbed well, but jammed
2. Compliant TPU rollers: fast and forgiving
3. Belt intake: worked, but too heavy

## Results

| Design | Avg. pickup time | Misses in 20 tries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claw (v1) | 3.1 s | 4 |
| Foam rollers | 1.2 s | 3 |
| TPU rollers | 0.8 s | 1 |
| Belt | 1.0 s | 1 |

## Decision

TPU rollers went on the robot as version v2.
