## What we tried

The main goal this meeting was to install the intake that had been assembled in meetings prior. An issue was fixed with the drivetrain, and the coding team wrote out the code for the intake dropping mechanism. 

## Results

Upon installation of the intake, it was found that the screw heads on the intake motor bevel gears prevented the motion, so the team decided to replace the screws with a button-head equivalent.
