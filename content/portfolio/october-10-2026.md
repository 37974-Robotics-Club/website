## Goal

As a good majority of the CAD had been finished prior, a large focus of the meeting was to start the assembly of the printed parts, working towards our first finished robot.  

## What we tried

The mechanical team's primary goal was to start the building of the robot, especially regarding the intake and drive chain, cutting side plates and drilling the top plate that will hold the shooter. While members worked on code, the uptake was assembled along with the intake rollers. 

## Decisions

Looking towards future meetings, members with 3D printers were allocated certain parts to print in their own time, ensuring that the parts could be ready in time for assembly.
