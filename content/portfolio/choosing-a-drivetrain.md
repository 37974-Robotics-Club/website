## Goal

Pick a drivetrain that lets the drive team line up with scoring zones quickly.

## Options

| Criterion (weight) | Tank | Mecanum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed (3) | 4 | 3 |
| Can strafe (3) | 1 | 5 |
| Pushing power (2) | 5 | 3 |
| Build difficulty (2) | 5 | 3 |
| **Weighted total** | **35** | **36** |

## Decision

Mecanum. Strafing matters more than pushing in this game.

## Test results

The finished base crossed the field in 2.4 seconds. The drive team liked field-centric controls after one practice.
